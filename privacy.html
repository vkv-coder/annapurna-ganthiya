Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dasaram Ganthiya & Bhajiya restaurant ordering app ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use it to place or manage restaurant orders. The App is built and hosted on the AnyApps.in platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to take and fulfill your order:

  • Name — to identify who placed an order.
  • Mobile Number — to identify staff accounts and, where applicable, to contact customers about an order.
  • Order History — items ordered, quantities, table/session details, and billing amounts.
  • Account Information — email address and login credentials, for restaurant owner and staff accounts.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used strictly for:

  • Processing and preparing food orders.
  • Generating bills and order records.
  • Restaurant staff account management (login and role access).
  • Operational notifications related to account approval and order handling.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the service providers strictly necessary to run the App (such as our database and authentication provider), solely to operate the service described above.

5. Data Storage & Security

Order and account data is stored securely using industry-standard hosted infrastructure. Access to restaurant data is restricted to the restaurant's own approved owner and staff accounts.

6. Data Retention

Order history and account data are retained for as long as needed for order processing, billing records, and legitimate business/accounting purposes, unless you request earlier deletion as described below.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or data requests, contact: vkvcoder.support@gmail.com

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
